How I work

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, heuristic reviews, analytics audits and competitive analysis — shared understanding before a single pixel is placed.

02

Define

Research synthesised into problem statements, personas and jobs-to-be-done — ambiguity turned into a north star.

03

Design

Sketching and IA through high-fidelity, design-system-aligned prototypes. Every decision tied to a need or a measurable outcome.

04

Validate

Usability tests, A/B experiments and accessibility audits before hand-off. Findings become design changes — not slide decks.

05

Scale

Post-launch monitoring, iteration sprints and design-system contributions that make the next product cheaper to build well.

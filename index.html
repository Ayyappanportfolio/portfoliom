How I Work

A process built for accountability, not ceremony.

The five-stage rhythm behind every project above.

01
Discover
Stakeholder interviews, heuristic reviews, analytics audits, and competitive analysis — building shared understanding before a single pixel is placed.
02
Define
Synthesising research into clear problem statements, personas, and jobs-to-be-done — turning ambiguity into a north star the whole team can design towards.
03
Design
From rapid sketching and information architecture through high-fidelity, design-system-aligned prototypes. Every decision tied to a need or a measurable outcome.
04
Validate
Usability testing, A/B experiments, and accessibility audits before hand-off. Findings become design changes — not slide decks.
05
Scale
Post-launch monitoring, iteration sprints, and design-system contributions that make the next product cheaper and faster to build well.
